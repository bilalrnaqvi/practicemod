# Hatch-start motion draft

6.4 seconds, starting crouched on all fours beside the hatch. No exterior climb,
stand-up or move behind the lid. The right hand lifts the handle through the first
30 degrees, then releases as the assisted lid completes its opening. The player
stays low, moves directly across the rim, lowers feet-first and transfers to the
side-wall indoor ladder. Existing seating/chair performance is retained.

Third-person review; shell cutaway begins at frame 70. Canopy glass is omitted.
These are preview presentation aids, not proposed in-game visibility changes.
Hand target errors are recorded in hand-contacts.json. Full-body collision, feet
and fine transition polish are not validated. No Minecraft behavior/model changes
in this revision. Earlier previews and sources are preserved.
