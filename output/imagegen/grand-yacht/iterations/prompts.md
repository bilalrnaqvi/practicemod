# Grand Yacht iterations

Built-in imagegen; original concept.png is the reference for all three. These are design explorations, not implemented models.

## Concept 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new landscape yacht concept sheet as a distinct design iteration of the attached approved Grand Yacht concept. Reference image role: visual family, huge inhabited scale, social purpose and presentation quality; redesign the architecture as directed below. Minecraft-native Blockbench-buildable cuboids and stepped hull shapes, crisp low-resolution pixel textures, blue pixel glass, deliberate broad surfaces. No photorealistic curved yacht meshes, fine noise, dense greebles or gratuitous bevels. Humongous player-owned yacht, roughly 60 blocks long with several fully walkable floors: lower hull guest accommodation, main indoor salon and outdoor party deck, upper terrace/bridge, rooftop lounge. Normal-size small Minecraft players demonstrate enormous scale. Clear full-height spaces and broad stairs connect levels, furniture leaves routes open. Socializing and hosting parties are the core purpose. Accessible NPC captain: right-click him, choose a water destination on a world map, captain slowly sails while guests move freely. No weapons, sails, helipad or racing controls.
Layout: large beautiful three-quarter exterior hero takes 70 percent of sheet; lower strip shows a consistent side cutaway with occupied lower hull, a small top-view reference, and a small captain/map interaction inset (coastline, yacht marker, sea route, destination pin, "Set destination"). Few short readable labels, no dimension annotations. Preserve consistency between views. Calm blocky ocean, polished art presentation.

Title "01 / WHITE PEARL". Focus strongly on WHITE and LUXURY. A timeless, palatial monohull superyacht with long terraced silhouette, predominantly clean pearl-white upper structure AND white hull sides, only a very thin dark boot stripe and charcoal window frames. Warm pale teak floors, ivory upholstered lounges, very restrained champagne-metal accents. Luxurious broad aft cascading terraces with wide symmetrical stairs framing a central lounge, upper rectangular pool, shaded dining veranda, roof daybeds, enormous picture-window salon. More sophisticated and spacious than reference; white horizontal ribbons unify the ship. Bright soft daylight, warm inset square lamps, turquoise water. Aft-quarter hero angle shows stern party terraces and long white flank with bow visible. Discrete integrated lighting instead of piles of lanterns.

Title "02 / WHITE HORIZON". Focus strongly on WHITE and LUXURY, but make this architecturally different from White Pearl or reference: a contemporary floating penthouse superyacht with an elongated stepped chisel bow, crisp cantilevered white roof planes, strongly recessed dark-blue glazing, broad white hull and minimal thin graphite waterline. Sculptural stepped cuboids ONLY, no smooth curves. A huge double-height central glazed salon/atrium with visibly usable upper gallery, connected to an open aft beach-club lounge and wide swim platform. Recessed rectangular infinity-edge pool on the aft main terrace, upper secluded dining terrace and forward private lounge. White built-in seating, pale limestone-like pixel surfaces, light wood and restrained satin silver. Strong luxury through spaciousness, clean architecture and very broad decks, no gold ornament. Maintain upper bridge and rooftop retreat plus real lower guest suites. Crisp midday lighting and pale aqua ocean. Forward-side three-quarter hero that also exposes the terraces; silhouette distinctly lower, longer and more architectural than reference.

Title "03 / NIGHT CURRENT". This third iteration deliberately ignores the request to emphasize white and luxury. Keep the original core: humongous habitable multi-level yacht with parties, guest accommodation and captain-driven slow map travel. Explore a bold dark floating music clubhouse: deep petrol-teal hull panels, charcoal structural base, copper-colored restrained trim, warm dark timber decks, amber interior light with sparse magenta and cyan party accents. Distinctive chunky stepped expedition-yacht silhouette, broad angular bow, sturdy framed windows, large sheltered open aft dance floor with a small music booth, connected upper viewing balconies, sociable roof terrace beneath square pergola beams, inviting indoor lounges and below-deck bunks/guest rooms. Comfortable, characterful and communal rather than a white luxury palace. It must still read as an enormous yacht, not a barge, cruise liner, military vessel or pirate ship. Dusk blue ocean, warm windows, readable vessel surfaces, a few guests dancing and mingling; restrained lighting, no nightclub haze. Rear-side three-quarter hero shows lively terraces and full long hull.
